## About Me

> **Pre-final year B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) student** at SRMIST, building AI systems at the intersection of ML, full-stack engineering, and embedded hardware. Currently deploying **Applivo**, a full-stack SaaS opportunity-orchestration platform, and actively pursuing SWE/ML internships.

- 🏆 **Top 1% — Amazon ML Challenge 2024** (Rank 62 / 6,223)
- 🥇 **CareHack'26 Winner** (AI/ML Track) · ₹30,000 cash prize · Internship @ Vdart
- 🥇 **YUVA National Hackathon Winner** (Hardware Track) · 🏅 **DigiGreen Hackathon — 4th Place**
- 📄 Co-authored an **IEEE paper** on an IoT-based backpack load alert system
- 🛠️ Systems Development Intern @ **C-DAC Chennai** · ML Intern @ **Prodigy InfoTech** (~40% runtime reduction) · **HackerRank Python Certified**
- 🔭 Currently diving deeper into **LLMs & Multimodal AI**

---

### 🧠 Machine Learning & Computer Vision

Train, evaluate, and optimize models in PyTorch, TensorFlow, OpenCV, and scikit-learn, from skin disease classification to student performance prediction and fraud detection.

### ⚙️ Backend & APIs

Serve models and business logic through FastAPI, Flask, and Node.js, backed by PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, and Firebase.

### 🖥️ Full-Stack Interfaces

Ship responsive products with React, Next.js, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind, including 3D and mapping interfaces with Cesium.

### 🔌 Embedded & IoT

Build hardware-backed systems with Arduino and Raspberry Pi, including a YUVA National Hackathon winning project and an IEEE-published IoT paper.

### ⚡ Deployment & Systems

Containerize with Docker Compose and deploy to Oracle Cloud ARM VMs, GCP, Vercel, and Render, with Linux systems experience from C-DAC.
